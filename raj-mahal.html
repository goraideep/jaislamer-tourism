About the site

The Raj Mahal is a tall palace of several storeys facing the main square of the fort. Its front is covered with jharokhas, the carved stone bay windows of Rajasthan, which let the royal women watch the square without being seen.

Inside, the Fort Palace Museum leads through painted rooms, small courtyards and the royal apartments, and shows weapons, costumes and the marble throne of the Maharawals. The roof has the best views over the fort and out across the desert.

History

The Maharawals built and rebuilt their palace over several centuries, adding new wings such as the Rang Mahal and the Sarvottam Vilas, which is decorated with blue tiles and glass mosaic.

The royal family later moved out of the fort to Mandir Palace in the town below. The old palace became a museum, run by a trust connected to the royal family.
