About the site

Laxminath Temple is the main Hindu temple inside Jaisalmer Fort. It is simpler than the Jain temples nearby, but its doorway is covered with embossed silver showing gods, animals and flowers. Inside, the images of Vishnu and Lakshmi are dressed and decorated by the temple priests every day.

The temple is still in daily worship. Visiting during the evening aarti, when lamps are lit and bells are rung, is a good way to see the living side of the fort.

History

The temple was built in the late 15th century, when the rulers of Jaisalmer chose Laxminath, a form of Vishnu, as a protector of the kingdom. The Maharawals are said to have ruled as the deity's representatives.

Over the years devotees and rulers added gifts of silver and gold, which is how the temple came to have its famous decorated doors.
