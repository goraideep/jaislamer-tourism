About the site

Gadisar Lake is a large reservoir surrounded by stone ghats, small shrines and chhatris standing on platforms in the water. Visitors enter through Tilon ki Pol, a carved gateway in golden sandstone, and can hire paddle boats or simply watch the catfish and pigeons at the steps.

At sunrise the fort can be seen glowing on the horizon, and in winter the lake attracts migratory birds. It is one of the most peaceful places in Jaisalmer.

History

Rawal Jaisal first dug a tank here, and in 1367 Maharawal Gadsi Singh rebuilt and enlarged it. Rainwater from the surrounding land collected in the lake, and for centuries it was the main water supply for the city.

The gateway, Tilon ki Pol, is said to have been built by Tilon, a wealthy courtesan. When the Maharawal refused to walk beneath a gate built by her, she added a temple of Krishna on top, so the gate could not be pulled down.
