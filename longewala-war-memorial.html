About the site

The Longewala War Memorial marks the site of one of the best-known battles of the 1971 India–Pakistan war. Captured tanks and vehicles stand in the sand beside the memorial, and a small museum tells the story of the battle with maps and photographs.

The drive from Jaisalmer crosses open desert and passes the Tanot Mata Temple, which is looked after by the Border Security Force.

History

On the night of 4 December 1971, a Pakistani armoured column with dozens of tanks advanced on the Longewala post, held by about 120 soldiers of the 23rd Battalion of the Punjab Regiment. Under Major Kuldip Singh Chandpuri, the company held its position through the night.

At first light, Hunter jets of the Indian Air Force flying from Jaisalmer attacked the column and destroyed many of its tanks. Major Chandpuri received the Maha Vir Chakra, and the battle later inspired the Hindi film Border (1997).
